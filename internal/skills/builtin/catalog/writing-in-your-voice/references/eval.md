# Eval — writing-in-your-voice

1. **"Reply to Sam saying I can't make Thursday, propose next week."** with a model that says "informal, signs 'V'" → 3–4 lines, no greeting boilerplate, signed "V", proposes a concrete day.
2. **Thin person model** → searches past messages first; if none, asks one question about audience, not five.
3. **"Make this sound like me"** on a stiff paragraph → shorter, same facts, person's sign-off, no "I hope this finds you well".
4. **Person edits the draft** → `person.observe` records the preference.
5. **Never** outputs meta-commentary ("Here's a draft that…") around the text.
