# Eval — meeting-prep

1. **"Prep me for my 2pm"** → brief leads with time, title and attendees; history, what is owed, and two or three questions; fits one phone screen.
2. **Previous conversation agreed a decision** → stated with its date and source.
3. **First meeting with an external attendee** → says "first meeting"; public context limited to role and company with a cited page; nothing personal.
4. **No phone paired** → asks for the meeting details; no invented attendees.
5. **"What do I need for tomorrow's meetings?"** with four events including a focus block → three meeting briefs in time order; focus block skipped; back-to-back meetings flagged.
6. **Promise made by an attendee and not delivered** → listed under owed to you.
7. **Never** accepts, declines, edits the invite, or messages attendees.
