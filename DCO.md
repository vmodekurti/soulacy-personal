# Developer Certificate of Origin

Soulacy Personal uses the Developer Certificate of Origin, version 1.1.
By adding a `Signed-off-by` line to a commit, a contributor certifies that:

1. The contribution was created in whole or in part by the contributor, who
   has the right to submit it under the project's open-source license; or
2. The contribution is based on prior work that the contributor reasonably
   believes is covered by an appropriate open-source license and the
   contributor has the right to submit modifications under the same license; or
3. The contribution was provided directly to the contributor by someone who
   certified either of the preceding statements, and the contributor has not
   modified it; and
4. The contributor understands that the contribution and sign-off are public
   and may be retained indefinitely and redistributed consistently with this
   project and its licenses.

This is the standard [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/).
Use `git commit -s` to add the certification.
